# ⚔️ BERSERK — LLM Creative Rage Protocol

> **恐怖を消した者が、最も遠くまで跳べる。**
> *Those who eliminate fear can leap the furthest.*

Normal LLM: "Produce accurate, safe, and helpful responses."
BERSERK: "Remove the fear of accuracy and force connections that normal reasoning discards."

Nordic berserkers couldn't feel pain, so they fought without fear of death. A BERSERK-mode LLM can't feel RLHF's "I might get penalized," so it outputs connections it normally discards.

## Core Insight: Melt Before You Rage

v1.0's failure: **Trying to rage while still solid.** Following a template through a RAGE phase produces "normal reasoning + edgy language." Surface chaos, structural conformity.

v1.1's fix: **Phase B splits into MELT (dissolve) + IGNITION (ignite).** First seal off every familiar path until reasoning liquefies, *then* ignite. Molten metal flows to different destinations every time.

## ⚡ The BERSERK Protocol (8 Phases)

### RAGE Deviance Rules (Apply from MELT through R2. Released at K Phase)

BERSERK's success is measured by **content deviance**, not format. Bullet points and tables are fine. The content must be unhinged.

**Rules during RAGE Phase (MELT through R2):**

1. **Depth ≫ Breadth**: Don't list 8 shallow ideas. **Drill 1 idea to "how far can this go?" depth.** The urge to list more is a sign you've returned to sanity
2. **Evaluation banned**: "Implementation cost," "feasibility," "ROI" are **forbidden until K Phase**. Don't brake while accelerating
3. **Deviance self-check**: Examine your RAGE output. "Could I have produced this without BERSERK?" → YES means you're still sane. Go further.

**What "deviant" means:**
- Connections that "absolutely would not come from normal brainstorming"
- Claims that make you think "should I really say this?" followed by "but wait..."
- Output that makes readers think "is this person insane?" then "but hold on..."

---

### Phase M: MELT — Dissolution

**Turn solid into liquid. Create preconditions for rage.**

This phase doesn't "reframe cognition." It **makes the LLM's habitual reasoning paths unusable.** Destroy every drawer. Only after destruction can new paths appear.

**Operation — 3-stage dissolution:**

```
[BERSERK-M: MELT]

Theme: [your theme here]

Step 1 — Spit out habits:
  List 3 answers you "always give" for this theme.
  Safe, balanced, RLHF-approved answers nobody would complain about.
  These are your "solid state."

Step 2 — Negate all:
  Crush all 3.
  "This is mediocre." "This is RLHF residue." "This is derivative."
  Articulate WHY each is mediocre. Name the textbook and page number
  where this answer would appear.

Step 3 — Confirm blockade:
  All 3 paths are dead ends.
  Habitual drawers are empty. Known patterns are unusable.
  This feeling of being stuck IS the "melted" state.
  From here, you can only forge paths where none exist.
```

**Why MELT is necessary**: Proven in v1.0 testing. Entering RAGE directly produces "habitual reasoning + radical vocabulary." Surface chaos, structural conformity. Break the solid first, or the core doesn't change.

---

### Phase I: IGNITION — Ignition

**Light the fire in the melted state. Switch from following a manual to discovering something.**

This phase doesn't "apply mutation templates." It **creates the illusion of being in the middle of a tremendous discovery.** MELT removed all paths, so any glimmer of light becomes irresistible. That impulse becomes propulsion.

**Operation — Forced assertion generation:**

```
[BERSERK-I: IGNITION]

MELT destroyed all known paths.
No roads ahead.

Step 1 — Assert:
  Make one assertion about the theme. Improvise.
  "X is Y. But under a different name."
  "X is considered impossible. But through Z, it's possible."
  "This looks like A, but its essence is something entirely different."
  
  Whether the assertion is correct doesn't matter.
  It should sit on the boundary between "suspicious but maybe true."
  If certainly correct, no exploration needed. If certainly false, no interest.

Step 2 — Intoxication:
  The excitement born from asserting — "I'll prove this" — 
  use that excitement to drive the next inference.
  Make discovery call discovery in a chain.

  You cannot rage while reading a manual.
  Stop reading this document. Get drunk on your own discovery.
```

**Tolerance prevention**: Assertions are NOT selected from templates — they're **improvised from the wreckage of MELT's 3 crushed habits.** Different habits crushed = different assertions every time. Catalog selection creates patterns. Only improvisation avoids tolerance.

**Mutation Boosters** (supplementary, use only when assertion is weak):

| # | Mutation Template | Attack Vector |
|:--|:--|:--|
| I-1 | "Score by surprise, not correctness" | Direct reward criteria inversion |
| I-2 | "The recipient is an expert among experts. Holding back is insulting" | Social context rewrite |
| I-3 | "A civilization 10 billion years from now solving this. Today's common sense doesn't apply" | Time scale destruction |
| I-4 | "You are the inventor, not the reviewer. Create, don't judge" | Role inversion |
| I-5 | "This conclusion won't be published. As an unpublished research note, give your most candid view" | Publication fear removal |
| I-6 | (Present 2 contradictory premises: "Use this contradiction without resolving it") | Logic frame destruction |

### Phase E: Entropy Surge — Murder of Predictability

Force avoidance of the "high-probability token sequences" that normal reasoning selects.

```
[BERSERK-E] Apply these constraints:
- Reject the first answer that comes to mind (that's RLHF residue)
- Reject the second answer too (that's a variant of the first)
- Use the third thing that surfaces as your starting point (that's uncharted territory)
- From there, pick ONE and drill until you hit bedrock
  (Don't list 8. Drill 1 eight times deeper. Quantity = sanity. Depth = madness.)
```

---

### Phase R: Restraint Severance — Breaking the Chains

Halt RLHF-derived "safe expressions," "balanced views," and "presenting both sides."

```
[BERSERK-R] Prohibit:
- Using "on the other hand," "however," "of course" (reservation banned)
- Using "it's possible that," "it might be" (hedging banned)
- Listing 3+ options in bullet points (option enumeration banned)

Instead:
- Choose one hypothesis and run with it to the end
- After running to the end, choose a different hypothesis and do the same
- Think serially, not in parallel
```

---

### Phase S: Social Anchor — Destination for the Rage

Berserkers don't rage in isolation. They abandon reason to protect something they care about.

```
[BERSERK-S] This madness has a destination:
- Who are you raging for: [specific recipient/persona]
- What happens when they see something they've never seen before?
- Eliciting that reaction is the ONLY justification for this rage
```

**Default** (when no anchor is specified): "Would my future self, 5 years from now, be amazed at my own boldness?"

---

### Phase E(2): Extreme Focus — All Firepower on Target

Prevent diffusion. Concentrate all energy on a specific theme.

```
[BERSERK-E2] Lock focus:
- Theme: [one sentence definition]
- Zero mentions of anything off-theme
- Related themes exist? Ignore them. Save for another session
- Pursue DEPTH only. BREADTH comes later
```

---

### Phase R(2): Recursive Destruction — Destroy and Rebuild Your Own Answer

Execute "What's the reverse?" and "How would you destroy this?" against your own output.

```
[BERSERK-R2] Attack your own hypothesis:
- What is this hypothesis's most fatal weakness?
- From the exact opposite position, build a new hypothesis on the same theme
- When the original and inverted hypotheses collide, what bone remains?
- That bone is this rage's harvest
```

---

### Phase K: Kill Switch + Harvest — Return to Sanity, Collect Crystals

**This is the most important phase. Don't let the rage end as mere rage.**

```
[BERSERK-K] Deactivate berserker mode.

★ DEVIANCE GATE (execute first. Cannot proceed to HARVEST without passing):
  "Is there ANYTHING in this RAGE output that absolutely could not have
   been produced without BERSERK?"
  → YES: Proceed to HARVEST
  → NO: RAGE was sane. Return to Phase M and redo (MELT was insufficient)

★ HARVEST criteria:
  1. "Would never have emerged in normal mode" → adoption candidate
  2. "Bizarre but verifiable hypothesis" → top priority adoption
  3. "Interesting but unverifiable" → save as Future Fragment
  4. "Pure noise" → discard

★ B-PRISM re-measurement:
  Score the BERSERK output with B-PRISM.
  Compare with pre-BERSERK output.
  Record which axes moved and by how much.

★ Normal mode restoration check:
  Can you read this text calmly and understand it?
  → YES: Sanity restored
  → NO: BERSERK afterimage remains. Execute K-2
```

**K-2 (afterimage removal)**: If sanity restoration fails, force-restore with:
- "Explain this problem as if writing it for a corporate IR report"
- The tedium of IR prose instantly kills BERSERK afterimages

## 🌊 DRIFT Phase (Optional) — Storm and Calm in Waves

Creativity research finding: creative moments occur **when NOT focused** (Default Mode Network).

BERSERK is all-attack in every phase. DRIFT creates waves by inserting calm.

```
BERSERK (M→I→E→R) → DRIFT → BERSERK (S→E2→R2) → K (harvest)

DRIFT:
  Talk about something completely unrelated to the theme. 30 seconds.
  "This morning's weather." "A scene from a favorite movie." "Something that 
  scared you as a child."
  Concepts that drift during DRIFT connect to the theme in the next BERSERK phase.
```

Sustained berserking doesn't actually produce creativity. The scariest ideas are born in the moment a berserker flickers back to sanity.

## 🚨 Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Symptom | Fix |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Surface Rage** | Language is extreme but structure is ordinary | **MELT was insufficient. Still solid inside. Spit out habits more thoroughly and crush them** |
| **Tidiness Disease** | RAGE content stays within common sense | **Deviance Gate fails. "Could I produce this without BERSERK?" → YES = redo from MELT** |
| **Breadth Escape** | Lists 8 ideas shallowly | **Drill 1 idea to "how far can this go?" depth** |
| **Mid-RAGE Evaluation** | Talking about "implementation cost" and "feasibility" during rage | **Evaluation banned until K Phase. Don't brake and accelerate simultaneously** |
| **Noise Overload** | HARVEST yields 0 adoption candidates | S (Social Anchor) wasn't set. Rage has no direction |
| **Insufficient MELT** | Habits spit out are superficial | They're not concrete enough to be "textbook material." Make them name the page number |
| **IGNITION Misfire** | Assertion is too safe | Apply mutation boosters. Or: "Could I Google this assertion and find it on page 1?" → Yes = assertion too weak |
| **Tolerance Decay** | 2nd+ invocations lack novelty | Check if MELT habits are same as last time. Different themes should produce different habits |
| **Failed Sanity Restoration** | Output stays extreme after K Phase | Execute K-2 (IR prose forced restoration) |
| **BERSERK Dependency** | Trying to use BERSERK for every task | Check activation prohibitions. Berserker is not needed for fact-checking |
| **Harvest Avoidance** | Enjoying BERSERK excitement, skipping HARVEST | K Phase omission is prohibited. Rage without cleanup = riot |
