# PRECOG PROTOCOL — Signal-Based Foresight Engine

> **Read scattered signals. Reverse-engineer the structure. Cut through bias. Judge timing on multiple axes. Design action.**

Not trend-following. **Trend-reading.** The difference is the same as reading sheet music vs. feeling the song.

---

## Theoretical Foundation

PRECOG PROTOCOL synthesizes three established strategic foresight methodologies into a single executable protocol for AI agents:

| Methodology | Origin | Core Insight | How PRECOG Applies It |
|-------------|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Horizon Scanning** | OECD, UNDP, UK Gov Office for Science | Systematically scan for early indicators of change across PESTEL dimensions | **Step 1 (Signal Map)** — structured signal collection with evidence and confidence tags |
| **Weak Signals Theory** | H. Igor Ansoff (1975) | Early, ambiguous hints of change are detectable before they crystallize into trends | **Step 2 (Convergence)** — connecting weak signals to reverse-engineer underlying structural shifts |
| **Scenario Planning** | Shell/Pierre Wack (1970s) | Don't predict one future; explore multiple plausible futures and prepare for each | **Step 3 (Contrarian)** — systematically challenging the dominant narrative to surface alternative futures |

**What makes PRECOG PROTOCOL different from these methodologies:**
These frameworks are designed for multi-week workshops with large teams. PRECOG compresses their core logic into a 5-step protocol executable by an AI agent in a single session, while adding two innovations:
1. **Timing Grid (Step 4)** — multi-axis timing judgment that no single framework addresses
2. **Signal History tracking** — longitudinal signal comparison across sessions, enabling cumulative foresight

## Confidence Tags (Mandatory on All Signals)

| Tag | Meaning | Criteria |
|-----|---------|----------|
| [Verified] | Confirmed fact | Official announcements, earnings reports, peer-reviewed papers, legislation |
| [Reported] | Multi-source reporting | Credible media coverage; primary source not directly accessed |
| [Speculative] | Hypothesis/inference | Extrapolation from individual cases, industry rumors, AI analysis |

### Evidence Chain (Inspired by MiroFish ReportAgent)

For each Signal and Convergence hypothesis, trace the reasoning chain:

```
Source → Inference → Conclusion
[Tag]     [Tag]       [Tag]
```

- **Source**: Raw data or event (e.g., "Q4 earnings report shows 30% decline")
- **Inference**: What the source implies (e.g., "Market appetite is shrinking")
- **Conclusion**: What follows for the prediction (e.g., "Correction expected in 2Q")
- **Chain confidence = lowest tag in the chain** (a [Verified] source + [Speculative] inference = [Speculative] conclusion)

Evidence Chains make hallucination-prone reasoning steps explicit and auditable.

## The Precog Protocol (5-Step Procedure)


- `goal` → Signal Mapのスコープ絞り込み（何についてのSignalを探すか）
- `current_state` → Timing Grid の Org Readiness（§4.3）を推測ではなく事実ベースで入力
- `constraints` → Timing Grid の External Window（§4.4）に外部制約を反映
- `stakeholders` → Action Window の WHO を導出


### Step 0: Subject Definition（分析対象の定義確認 — 省略禁止）

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Signal Mapを構築する**前に**、分析対象が何であるかをオーナーに確認する。分析者の思い込みで対象を定義すると、後続の全ステップが汚染される（2026-03-25 実証済み）。

Procedure:
1. **What it IS**: 分析対象の正確な定義を1-2文で記述
2. **What it is NOT**: 誤認されやすいカテゴリを明示的に除外
3. **Owner confirmation**: 上記の定義をオーナーに確認。不一致があればここで修正

Format:
```
## Step 0: Subject Definition
- **IS**: [正確な定義]
- **IS NOT**: [除外するカテゴリ]
- **Confirmed by**: [Owner / Assumed（未確認の場合）]
```

> 「Assumed」のまま進んだ分析は、Contrarian View で定義の誤認を Collapse trigger に含めること。

### Step 1: Signal Map (3-5 Observable Signals)

Collect 3-5 **observable signals** related to the target theme.

Each signal requires:
- **Signal name**: One-line description of what's happening
- **Evidence**: Specific facts, data, or examples (with sources)
- **Direction**: ↑ Accelerating / → Stable / ↓ Decelerating
- **Confidence tag**: [Verified] / [Reported] / [Speculative]

Where to look for signals:
- **Numeric changes** — revenue, hiring, search volume, patent filings
- **Behavioral changes** — major player entries/exits, talent migration, investment shifts
- **Narrative changes** — media tone, conference topics, job posting language
- **Absent signals** — "Why *isn't* this happening?" is often the most important signal

---

### Step 2: Convergence Analysis (Reverse-Engineering Structure)

Identify where multiple signals **intersect** and reverse-engineer the underlying structure.

Process:
- "Signal A + Signal C happening simultaneously → This structural shift is behind it"
- "Signal B and Signal D are moving in opposite directions → Tension/inflection point here"
- "Signal E is absent → This assumption is breaking down"

Narrow to 1-3 convergence points. For each:
- **Structural hypothesis**: What's happening (one sentence)
- **Causal logic**: Why these signals connect
- **Confidence**: High / Medium / Low + rationale

---

### Step 2.5: Perspective Split (Multi-Viewpoint Stress Test)

Before challenging the mainstream view, stress-test Convergence hypotheses from **3-5 distinct viewpoints**.

This step is inspired by MiroFish's multi-agent simulation: instead of one analyst reviewing one conclusion, multiple viewpoint simulations evaluate the same hypothesis from different angles.

#### Perspective Selection

Pick 3-5 from this roster (or create domain-specific ones):

| Perspective | Lens | Typical Bias |
|-------------|------|--------------|
| 🏢 CEO / Investor | Growth, ROI, market share | Overestimates opportunity size |
| 🏛️ Regulator / Policy | Compliance, public interest | Overestimates regulatory friction |
| 🔧 Engineer / Builder | Technical feasibility, cost | Underestimates market demand |
| 🛒 Consumer / End User | Value, convenience, trust | Short-term thinking |
| 📰 Journalist / Analyst | Narrative, trend, framing | Recency bias, hype amplification |
| 🎲 Contrarian / Outsider | "What if everyone is wrong?" | Contrarian for its own sake |

#### Procedure

For each Convergence hypothesis:
1. Each perspective writes **1 paragraph** evaluating the hypothesis through their lens
2. After all perspectives, extract:
   - **Agreement zone**: Points all perspectives support
   - **Fault lines**: Points where perspectives sharply disagree + why
   - **Blind spots**: Topics no perspective mentioned (these are the most dangerous)

Format:
```
## Perspective Split: [Hypothesis]
| Perspective | Assessment | Key Concern |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| 🏢 CEO | "Bullish — $X TAM is underpriced" | Ignores regulatory risk |
| 🏛️ Regulator | "Caution — compliance gap exists" | Overestimates enforcement speed |
| 🔧 Engineer | "Feasible but 18 months, not 6" | Optimistic on talent supply |

→ Agreement: [Market is real]
→ Fault line: [Timeline — 6mo vs 18mo, CEO vs Engineer]
→ Blind spot: [No one discussed supply chain risk]
```

**The biggest Fault Line feeds directly into Step 3 (Contrarian View).**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Perspective Split is MANDATORY for every PRECOG execution.** It is HA's distinctive multi-viewpoint stress test (inspired by MiroFish). Do NOT skip.
> - **Risk ≥ M or prediction horizon ≥ 6 months**: Full split (3-5 perspectives)
> - **Risk = L, horizon < 6 months**: Minimum 2 perspectives (most bullish + most bearish)

### Step 3: Contrarian View (Bias Elimination)

For the most "obvious" conclusion, examine **why it might be wrong**.

Required:
- At least one reason it may be overestimated
- A historical analogy where a **similar setup led to a different outcome** (if available)
- **Preconditions**: "This hypothesis is valid only if [A] and [B] hold true"
- **Collapse scenario**: Which precondition, if broken, invalidates the entire thesis

Format:
```
🔴 Contrarian: [Mainstream view] may be wrong because:
- Reason 1: ...
- Reason 2: ...
- Historical parallel: [Case] → [Actual outcome]
- Preconditions: Valid only if [A] AND [B] hold
- Collapse trigger: If [C] occurs, this thesis fails
```

### Step 4: Timing Grid (Multi-Axis Timing Judgment)

"When to move" cannot be determined on a single axis. **Layer multiple timing dimensions.**

#### 4.1 Market Phase (Hype Cycle Axis)

| Phase | Characteristics | Guideline |
|-------|----------------|-----------|
| 🌱 Emergence | Experimental, low awareness | Research & small experiments |
| 📈 Acceleration | Rapid adoption, investment surge | Aggressive entry |
| 🏔️ Peak Hype | Overinflated expectations, bubble-like | Caution, focus on differentiation |
| 📉 Correction | Disillusionment, shakeout | Winner selection, bargain entry |
| 🏗️ Plateau | Established, mature | Efficiency optimization |

#### 4.2 Competitive Timing Axis

| Position | Meaning | Decision Criteria |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| 🏃 First Mover | Move first | Market-defining opportunity, high risk |
| 🎯 Fast Follower | Leverage first mover's lessons | Enter right after market is proven |
| 🏰 Fortifier | Late but resourced to dominate | Enter after winning patterns emerge |
| ⏳ Too Late | Window has closed | Retreat or niche-specialize |

#### 4.3 Organizational Readiness Axis

| Level | State | Required Action |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| 🔴 Not Ready | Missing people/tech/capital | Build foundation first |
| 🟡 Partially Ready | Some pieces in place | Fill gaps in parallel with small starts |
| 🟢 Ready | Can execute now | Go |

#### 4.4 External Window Axis

Often overlooked but critical:
- **Regulatory window**: Pre/post legislation or deregulation
- **Capital window**: Fundraising market temperature (cold vs. overheated)
- **Seasonal/Event**: Industry conferences, fiscal year budget cycles, competitor release timing
- **Technology maturity**: GA (General Availability) date of dependency technologies

#### 4.5 Timing Grid Output

```
| Axis | Current Position | Judgment |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Market Phase | 📈 Acceleration | Go-leaning |
| Competitive | 🎯 Fast Follower | Go |
| Org Readiness | 🟡 Partially Ready | Conditional Go |
| External Window | Regulation easing Q2 2026 [Reported] | Soon |
| → Overall | **Conditional Go** — [Explicit conditions] |
```

### Step 5: Action Window

Based on the Timing Grid, specify **when to do what**.

Three categories:
- **Now**: Low-cost actions to start immediately (1-2)
- **Soon**: Invest when conditions are met (1-2)
- **Watch**: Don't move yet, but trigger on these signals (1-2)

Each action requires:
- **WHO**: Responsible person/team
- **WHAT**: Specific action
- **TRIGGER**: Execution condition (especially for Soon/Watch)
- **COST**: Estimated cost/resources (if known)

### Step 2: Convergence Analysis

**Convergence 1: "Tool fatigue + individual gap = opportunity for opinionated, personal frameworks"**

- Confidence: **High** — consistent signal across multiple developer communities

**Convergence 2: "Hype peak + enterprise surge = correction is 6-12 months away"**
- Signals 2 + 5 converge: Enterprise adoption is surging AT the hype peak, which historically precedes a correction phase
- Confidence: **Medium** — timing uncertain, could be 6-18 months

### Step 3: Contrarian View

🔴 **Contrarian: "AI agents are not at hype peak — they're at genuine inflection"**
- **Reason 1:** Unlike blockchain/metaverse hype cycles, AI agents deliver measurable productivity gains (GitHub data: 55% faster coding with Copilot). Real utility slows down the correction.
- **Reason 2:** Enterprise adoption at 65% piloting is unusually high for "hype" — usually hype peaks at 20-30% piloting.
- **Historical parallel:** Cloud computing (2010-2012) — appeared to be "hype" but was actually genuine infrastructure shift. Correction was mild and brief.
- **Preconditions:** This thesis holds IF productivity gains sustain AND cost per token continues declining.
- **Collapse trigger:** If a major AI agent failure causes regulatory backlash (e.g., autonomous agent causes financial loss), correction could be severe.

## Anti-Patterns (How This Goes Wrong)

| Anti-Pattern | Symptom | Fix |
|-------------|---------|-----|
| **Confirmation Bias in Signal Selection** | Only collecting signals that support a pre-existing belief | Force at least one signal that contradicts your thesis. If you can't find one, your search is too narrow |
| **Single-Axis Timing** | "Market is hot, let's go!" without checking readiness or competition | Always complete the full 4-axis Timing Grid. One "Go" axis doesn't mean overall "Go" |
| **Vague Action Windows** | "Watch and see" / "Monitor the situation" without specific triggers | Every Watch item MUST have a concrete trigger: "If X happens, then do Y" |
| **Recency Bias** | Overweighting events from the last 2 weeks | Check: "Would I still select this signal if it happened 6 months ago?" |
| **Survivorship Bias** | Only citing successful cases in Convergence | In Contrarian View, actively search for failures in similar situations |
| **Speculative Masquerading as Verified** | Marking rumor-based signals as [Verified] | Strict rule: [Verified] = primary source accessible. If you can't link to the source, it's [Reported] at best |
| **Identity Misread** | 分析者が分析対象の本質を誤認し、全ステップが汚染される（例: 「AIオーケストレーション研究」を「個人向けツール」と誤分類） | **Step 0を省略するな。** オーナーに定義を確認してから分析を開始。Assumed定義で進んだらContrarian ViewのCollapse triggerに「定義の誤認」を含める |

## Guardrails (Quality Gates)

- **Fact Gate enforced**: Signal analysis is a hallucination hotspot. [Verified] is for primary sources only.
- **Time sensitivity**: Every "latest" claim must have a date attached. Unknown date = [Reported] or below.
- **Prediction humility**: Future predictions must carry [Estimate] or [Speculative] tags. Never state predictions as fact.
- **Bias awareness**:
  - Recency bias (overvaluing what just happened)
  - Survivorship bias (only seeing winners)
  - Confirmation bias (finding what you want to find)
  → Contrarian View (Step 3) is the mandatory antidote
- **Multi-axis judgment**: Never decide timing on one axis alone. Always use the full Timing Grid.

| 6 | 📰 Narrative Journalist | Story, trend, public perception | Technical depth, edge cases |
| 7 | 👻 **Ghosty Wildcard** | _Generated via GHOSTY COLLIDER_ | _By design: fills the group's collective blind spot_ |

**Viewpoint 7 is mandatory.** It is generated by running Ghost Extraction on the prediction theme and finding the perspective that _no one in the room would naturally hold_.

### Procedure

```
1. Each viewpoint simulation writes 1 paragraph on the prediction theme
   - Each simulates a distinct perspective (NOT truly independent samples)
   - Each uses their lens to evaluate probability + timeline + impact

2. META-ANALYST aggregates:
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Agreement Map: Points 5+ viewpoints agree│
   │ Fault Map:     Points with 2+ vs 2+     │
   │ Distribution:  Scenario × Probability    │
   │ Blind Spots:   Topics NO viewpoint raised│
   └─────────────────────────────────────────┘

3. Fault Map's biggest rift → feeds into Step 3 (Contrarian View)
4. Blind Spots → added to Signal Map as [Speculative] signals
```

### Output Format

```markdown
## 🐟 SWARM PRECOG: [Theme]

### Viewpoint Predictions
| # | Viewpoint | Prediction | Probability | Timeline |
|---|---------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | 🏢 CEO | "Rapid consolidation" | 70% | 12-18mo |
| 2 | 🏛️ Regulator | "Regulation slows growth" | 40% | 24mo |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 7 | 👻 Wildcard | "[Unexpected angle]" | ??% | ??mo |

### Meta-Analysis
- **Agreement (5+/7)**: [What most viewpoints agree on — note: directional heuristic, not statistical evidence]
- **Fault Line**: [Biggest disagreement + why it matters]
- **Probability Distribution**: [Scenario A: X%, Scenario B: Y%]
- **Blind Spots**: [What NO one mentioned → added as new signals]
```

### Cost

- 7 viewpoints × ~200 tokens each + meta-analysis ≈ 2,500 tokens total
- Roughly 3× the cost of a standard precog analysis
- Worth it for Risk ≥ M predictions with 1+ year horizon
